How to post a seamless panorama on Instagram

  1. Drop in a wide photo. Swipeframe suggests the slide count that keeps the most of it.
  2. Pick 4:5 for the tallest slides in the feed, or 1:1 for squares.
  3. Slide the crop position until the horizon sits where you want it.
  4. Download the zip and unzip it on your phone.
  5. In Instagram, tap +, choose Select multiple, and tap the files in order: 01, 02, 03…
  6. Post. Followers swipe across one continuous image.

Questions

How many slides can a carousel have?
Instagram allows up to 20 photos per carousel, so Swipeframe goes up to 20.
Which size is best?
4:5 at 1080 × 1350 px takes up the most screen in the feed. Every slide is exported at exactly that size, so Instagram doesn't recompress the edges.
What's the profile-grid mode?
It splits one image across 3, 6, 9 or more posts so your profile shows it as one big picture. Upload post-01 first; it ends up at the bottom right.
Is my photo uploaded anywhere?
No. All cutting happens in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
